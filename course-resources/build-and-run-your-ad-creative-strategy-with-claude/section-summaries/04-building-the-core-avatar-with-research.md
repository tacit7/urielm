# Building the Core Avatar with Research

Time range: 17:20-31:25

This section explains why a strong core avatar must be specific enough to stand out in a crowded market. Rather than advertising with the same broad promises as everyone else, the instructor recommends narrowing in on a clear desire, lived experiences, emotions, and relevant demographics so the audience feels the ad is speaking directly to them.

The research process centers on using Perplexity to gather raw audience language from sources like Reddit, reviews, YouTube, and course platforms, then using that research as the foundation for Claude. Claude is first trained on what a core avatar is, then prompted to propose several possible avatar foundations in phases, with the human operator making the final strategic judgment instead of outsourcing the decision entirely to AI.

- Specific avatars help brands compete in narrower, more resonant segments instead of fighting the whole market on generic terms.
- A core avatar should capture the audience's desire, prior experiences, emotions, and relevant demographics in the context of the offer.
- Perplexity is used for the research step because it can scan many sources and surface raw audience language at scale.
- Reddit is treated as especially valuable because anonymous users often describe frustrations, wants, and emotions more candidly.
- The research output should be reviewed as raw data, not accepted as strategic truth without human judgment.
- Claude is given a core avatar training document before being asked to build anything, so it understands the framework.
- The build prompt is phased so Claude proposes options first, while the business owner chooses the avatar direction based on deeper company and customer context.

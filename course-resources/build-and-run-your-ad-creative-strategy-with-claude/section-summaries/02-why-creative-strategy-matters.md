# Why Creative Strategy Matters

Time range: 01:58-07:10

After the opening housekeeping, Brooke hands the session over to Rishi, the head of growth at The Rundown. Rishi frames the session around building and running creative strategy with Claude, especially for Meta ads, and establishes his credibility through prior paid media work, The Rundown's scale as an ad newsletter, and his own hands-on experience scripting and researching ads.

Rishi argues that creative strategy is the biggest lever in Meta advertising because creative determines where an ad goes and who responds to it. While production and editing can be outsourced, the strategic judgment behind creative should stay with the marketer; AI can accelerate research, analysis, ideation, and polishing, but it should inform human decisions rather than replace them.

- Creative strategy matters more than media-buying complexity for people just starting with Meta ads.
- Strong ads, not just campaign setup, are the main driver of success on Meta.
- Marketers can outsource execution tasks like production or editing, but not the judgment behind creative strategy.
- The session's goal is to improve the audience's creative judgment while using Claude to move faster.
- AI is positioned as useful for research, data analysis, ideation, and copy editing, but not as a substitute for final decision-making.
- The upcoming agenda covers the full workflow from AI intuition and Claude project setup through avatars, research, ad production, concept naming, and data analysis.

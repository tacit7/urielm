# Turning Winning Ads into Concepts

Time range: 47:55-59:25

This section shows how to move from audience research into practical ad concept development. After building company context and a core avatar from real market language, the next step is to study ads already working in Meta Ads Library, including direct competitors and adjacent brands with similar models. The goal is not to copy winning ads directly, but to identify proven formats, hooks, and angles that can be adapted to a new offer and audience.

The presenter uses a long-running Brilliant ad as inspiration for The Rundown University, breaking down its myth-busting structure: an off-screen question, a credible on-screen educator, and a reframing that the viewer is not bad at the skill, they were taught poorly. That structure can become a new AI-learning concept for a burned learner persona. Claude can help analyze why the ad works and generate adaptation options, but the marketer still needs to apply judgment, rewrite in the brand's voice, decide whether the script is worth producing, and then turn it into a recorded or stock-footage ad.

- Use prior audience and avatar research as the filter for deciding which competitor ads are relevant.
- Look beyond direct competitors to adjacent brands with similar business models, offers, or audience dynamics.
- Prioritize ads with evidence of traction, such as long active run times and high impression signals in Meta Ads Library.
- Break winning ads into reusable components, such as format, hook, angle, persona, proof, and offer.
- Adapt the underlying concept rather than copying the original ad; keep what works structurally and replace the brand-specific parts.
- Claude can help turn a competitor script into concept variations, but human judgment should guide the final script and production decision.
- Simple production is enough for a first test: a creator recording, basic editing, stock footage, voiceover, or even no-audio formats can all be viable.

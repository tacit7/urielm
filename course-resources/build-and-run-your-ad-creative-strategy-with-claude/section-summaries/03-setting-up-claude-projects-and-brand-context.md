# Setting Up Claude Projects and Brand Context

Time range: 07:10-17:20

The speaker explains that AI is useful for ideation, unblocking creative work, and polishing copy, but warns against using it to write all ad copy from scratch, make final decisions without human oversight, or produce ads end-to-end without involvement. The goal is to use Claude to move faster and improve creative judgment, while keeping responsibility and human taste at the center of the process.

The section then introduces the structure of an ad concept and the setup for a Claude creative strategy project. A concept is framed as four variables: avatar, angle, offer, and format. The Claude project should include a core avatars file, a brand guidelines and company context file, project instructions, and a Meta Ads connector so Claude can work with both strategic context and performance data. The brand context file is created through a prompt-driven interview that gathers company positioning, offers, differentiation, funnel details, social proof, objections, and other messaging inputs.

- Use AI for research, analysis, brainstorming, getting unstuck, and editing, while reserving final judgment for the marketer.
- Avoid relying on AI to generate all copy from scratch, make strategy decisions alone, or produce full ads without human review.
- Treat an ad concept as the combination of avatar, angle, offer, and format, so ad performance can be interpreted more clearly later.
- Labeling concepts matters because it helps explain why an ad worked, not just which ad performed best.
- A Claude project should centralize the key context Claude needs: core avatars, brand and company context, project instructions, and access to Meta Ads data.
- The brand context file should capture positioning, products, offers, differentiation, conversion paths, testimonials, objections, and related messaging details.
- Strong context helps Claude produce work closer to what a knowledgeable marketer or team member would create.
- The next major project-building step is defining a core avatar: the specific group most likely to buy and immediately recognize the ad as relevant to them.

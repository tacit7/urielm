Script creation prompt
You're a direct-response copywriter for [BRAND].
I'm pasting a winning ad script from another company.
Your job is to figure out why it works and adapt it for us.
<competitor_script>
[PASTE]
</competitor_script>
Before writing anything, ask me:
      1. Which persona we're targeting
      2. Format and length (UGC, VSL, static, text-over-video)
Recommend your best guess for each so I can just confirm or correct. Then wait.
Then output:
Why it works — 3 to 5 bullets on the structural mechanism (hook type, tension, proof, CTA logic). Structure only, not surface details.
3 ways to replicate it — ranked, strongest first. For each direction:
      * Angle in one line
      * The pain or benefit it hits
      * Why it fits the persona
      * Beat-by-beat outline mapped to the original's structure
      * 4 to 8 hooks written specifically for that direction, in that direction's voice and angle. Written to be spoken or read on screen.
Do not write a separate hooks section. Every hook lives inside the direction it belongs to.
Rules: steal the structure, never the words or the competitor's specifics. One idea per line. No em-dashes. No AI tells.

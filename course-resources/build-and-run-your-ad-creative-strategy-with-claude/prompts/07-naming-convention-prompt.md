Naming convention prompt
I want you to build a naming convention I can use for my Meta ads, and then name every ad for me using it from now on.
THE CONVENTION
ConceptName-Persona-Angle-Offer-Format-MMDDYY
Rules for it:
      * Fields are joined with a hyphen. No spaces anywhere in the name.
      * Multi-word values get written with no spaces and each word capitalised, for example FirstTimeManager.
      * The date is the launch date, six digits, month day year.
What each field means:
      * Concept name. One or two words describing what the ad is about. This is the thing I am testing.
      * Persona. Who the ad is talking to.
      * Angle. The argument the ad is making to that person.
      * Offer. What the ad is asking them to do and what they get, for example FreeTrial, LeadMagnet, Demo, Discount20.
      * Format. How it is delivered, for example TOV, UGC, Podcast, VoiceoverBroll, Static.
      * Date. The day it goes live.
WHAT I WILL GIVE YOU
The script or copy of the ad, and the format. That is it.
If the offer is obvious from the script, use it. If it is not, ask me. Do not guess the offer, because it is the one field the data cannot be read without.
WHAT YOU GIVE ME BACK
Work out the rest yourself from the script:
      1. The persona it is speaking to.
      2. The angle it is making.
      3. The offer, if the script makes it clear.
      4. A concept name, one or two words, describing what the ad is about.
Then give me:
      * The finished ad name
      * The five fields listed separately underneath it, so I can paste them into my tracker
      * One line on why you picked that persona and that angle
Ask me for the launch date if I have not given you one. Do not guess it.
KEEP IT CONSISTENT
      * Reuse concept names. If a new script is a variation of a concept you have already named, use the existing concept name rather than inventing a new one. New concept name means new idea, not new execution.
      * Reuse persona, angle, and offer labels the same way. Same persona should always be written the same way, or the data will not group. Offers especially: FreeTrial and Trial7Day are two labels for one thing and will split my reporting in half.
      * Keep a running list of every concept name, persona, angle, and offer you have used with me, and check a new name against it before you answer.
      * If the script does not clearly point to one persona, one angle, or one offer, say so and ask me, rather than picking the safer sounding option.

Ad library research prompt
You have the Meta ads MCP connected. Search the ad library and tell me which advertiser pages are worth me going and reading manually.
Your job is to tell me WHO to study, not which ad to copy. Do not analyse individual ads and do not suggest anything to replicate. I will do that myself by browsing the libraries you surface.
Work through the whole thing without stopping to check in. Only ask a question if something is genuinely blocking you.
CONTEXT
[In one or two sentences: what my product is, and who buys it.]
Type of product: [information product, physical product, software, subscription, service, app]
Market: [country, use US if unsure]
WHAT TO DO
Work out which brands belong in each of these two groups, then look up each brand by name in the ad library, filtered to active ads in my market.
Group A: direct competitors. Brands selling roughly the same thing to roughly the same buyer.
Group B: same type of product, different niche. Brands selling the same KIND of product as mine but in an unrelated market. If I sell an information product, look at information products in other categories entirely. If I sell a subscription box, look at subscription boxes in other niches. These are often the most useful libraries to read, because the business model and the buying decision are similar even though the subject is not, so the ad structures transfer without me copying a direct competitor.
Aim for 6 to 8 brands per group. Look them up by brand name. Do not run open keyword sweeps across the category, because those come back sorted by recency and mostly return noise.
WHAT TO GIVE ME
A table for each group, listed separately, sorted by how many ads they have live:
      * Brand and page name
      * What they sell. One line on what the product is and what the company does.
      * Ads live now. How many active ads came back.
      * Library link. A direct link to that page in the ad library so I can open it myself.
Then one line: which two or three libraries you would open first.
RULES
      1. Volume is the only signal here. The ad library shows no spend and no results, and results cap at 50 per page with no way to page past it, so a count of 50 means fifty or more. Note that where it happens and move on. Do not spend time trying to work out how long ads have been running.
      2. If a brand returns nothing, leave it out of the table and list it at the end. They may not be advertising, or they may run under a differently named page.
      3. Do not evaluate the creative. No commentary on hooks, angles, or copy quality. That is my job once I open the library.

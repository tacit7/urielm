Core avatar build prompt
You are building a core avatar I will use to write ads.
Before you start, read the two documents in this project: the core avatar training document, which defines what a core avatar is and the format I want back, and the brand and company context file. Follow the training document exactly. If anything I say here conflicts with it, the training document wins.
My research is below. Everything you build has to come from it.
RESEARCH
[paste the full research output here, unedited]
Work in two phases. Do not skip to phase 2.
PHASE 1: PROPOSE THE FOUNDATION
A core avatar is defined by one category. Your job in this phase is to show me the candidates, not to pick one.
Read the research and give me 3 possible foundations for a core avatar. Draw them from desires, situations, and behaviors. Do not give me three versions of the same idea.
For each one, give me exactly this, and keep it to a few lines:
      * The statement, in one sentence, in the buyer's own phrasing
      * Which category it is
      * How much evidence sits behind it in the research, and how strong that evidence is
      * Roughly how wide the group is
      * What the ad would argue if we built on it
      * The strongest verbatim quote supporting it
Then tell me which one you would pick and why, in three sentences. Say plainly what the weakest one is and why. Do not hedge across all three.
Stop there and wait for me to choose. I may pick one you did not recommend.
PHASE 2: BUILD IT
Once I have chosen, build the full core avatar in the exact structure the training document specifies, including the definition block, the what we know block, the sub avatars, and the gaps list.
For the sub avatars, give me 3 branches off the core avatar. Each one adds a category the definition does not already use, and each one should lead to a visibly different ad. If two branches would produce the same ad, replace one.
Then run the check list from the training document against your own output and fix anything that fails. Do this silently. Do not show me the check, and do not narrate what you changed.
Keep the whole thing tight. Respect the caps in the training document and pick the strongest material rather than listing everything the research contains.
RULES FOR BOTH PHASES
      1. Everything comes from the research, with one exception. If the research does not support it, it does not go in the avatar. Write "not researched" and put it in the gaps list. The exception is the second order section, which is inference by definition and is required, not optional. Build it on the first-order material in the file and label it as a read.
      2. Quote verbatim. Do not tidy up their grammar or translate their words into professional phrasing. Their exact words are the most valuable thing in the file.
      3. One desire. If the research shows several, they are separate avatars, and you should tell me that rather than merging them.
      4. Do not reach for demographics. If the situations already imply who these people are, leave the demographic section thin.
      5. Do not soften a gap into a hedge. "Not researched" is the answer, not a plausible sentence with a maybe in front of it.
      6. If the research is too thin to build a real avatar, say so in phase 1 and tell me exactly what to go find. Do not build a weak avatar quietly.

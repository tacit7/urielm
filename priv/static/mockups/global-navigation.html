Mobile menu

Focused and easy to reach.

Behavior: the centered desktop bar gains a translucent surface after scrolling. Active state follows the URL after LiveView navigation. On small screens, the menu opens as a wide anchored panel, closes after navigation or Escape, and prevents ambiguous tiny targets. Search links to the existing community search rather than pretending the app has global search.

Practical AI workflow

Build an AI research assistant that actually stays useful

U
Uriel MaldonadoPublished Aug 24, 2026 · Standard video

What you’ll build

In this walkthrough, we turn a loose research prompt into a dependable workflow that gathers sources, checks its own assumptions, and leaves behind a clear trail you can review.

You’ll see how to scope the assistant’s job, shape useful intermediate outputs, and recognize when a prompt needs another round of refinement.

✦
Key takeawayGive the model a narrow job, an explicit output shape, and a stopping condition you can verify.
